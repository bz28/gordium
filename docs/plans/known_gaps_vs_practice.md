# Known gaps compared with how architects really work

**Purpose:** what architects look into on a real project that Gordium doesn't yet, each with a proposed next step, so nobody mistakes Gordium's scope for the whole job.\
**For:** Ben, the architects, shareholders.\
**Kept current by:** the coordinator, whenever a gap is closed (move it to the System Map's "built" list) or a new one is found.

Gordium today checks a house's **zoning** (its size, height and distance from the lot lines) in the City of Ithaca's residential [districts](../glossary.md#district). An architect's real job covers more. Each gap below says what is missing and the proposed next step; none is decided.

| # | Gap | Why it matters | Proposed next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Parking** is out of scope for now (with other interior items such as bedrooms) | Where the cars go is a site decision, made outside the house, so it belongs with the exterior work | Bring parking back in scope as a site item: spaces drawn on the lot, counted against the chart |
| 2 | **Zoning only.** The building code, historic review, site plan review and easements are not checked | A reader could take "complies" to mean the project will get its permit | Say so in the Rhino panel: "Zoning only: building code, historic review, site plan review and easements are not checked" |
| 3 | **Variances.** A design that breaks a rule just fails | Architects sometimes ask the [BZA](../glossary.md#bza) for a [variance](../glossary.md#variance); knowing how similar requests went helps them decide | Flag "would need a variance" on a failing item, with past BZA decisions on the same rule |
| 4 | **No survey yet.** Early designs use the tax-map lot (about ±2 ft) | A real project starts from a boundary survey; a 2 ft error can decide a yard | Nudge "load your survey" when a project moves past early design, and mark tax-map results as approximate (already shown) |
| 5 | **FEMA flood zones** are not looked up | Building in a flood zone has its own rules | Add the federal flood map to the address lookup (public data, may be stored; see [data sources](../decisions/2026-10-20_data_sources_and_permissions.md)) |
| 6 | **Not yet tried on a real project with the architects** | Every test so far is the sample lot or designs we drew | Run one live project with the architects, start to finish, and record where Gordium helped or got in the way |
