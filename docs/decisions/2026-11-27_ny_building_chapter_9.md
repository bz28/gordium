# New York's Building Code Chapter 9, the parts egress turns on: the plan (2026-11-27)

**Purpose:** which sections of Chapter 9 (fire protection and life safety systems) of the 2025 Building Code of New York State Gordium brings in for egress, what is a rule and what a flag, the building facts they read, where the rules go, and the order of the PRs.\
**For:** engineers extending `new_york_state_building/`; Ben (scope, order); reviewers of each part's PR.\
**Kept current by:** the engineer who drafts a part, in that same branch (the part's status line below).

Follows [Chapter 10's plan](2026-11-23_ny_building_egress_all_occupancies.md) and [Chapter 7's](2026-11-26_ny_building_chapter_7.md). Chapter 10 gives many allowances to a building "equipped throughout with an automatic sprinkler system in accordance with Section 903.3.1.1 or 903.3.1.2" (Tables 1006.2.1, 1017.2 and 1020.2, 1007.1.1, 1005.3's smaller factors), and some to one with an emergency voice/alarm system per 907.5.2.2. Until now Gordium took the sprinkler system as the architect's statement (`sprinkler_system`: none, NFPA 13, 13R or 13D) and never asked whether the law requires one. Chapter 9 says where a system is required (903.2), which system may be used (903.3.1), and where a fire alarm and an emergency voice/alarm system are required (907.2). The same pipeline as Chapters 7 and 10: sources.yaml, extraction, every number read a second time from the page images, labels, rules or flags, coverage.

## Decisions

1. **One rulebook file.** Chapter 9's rules go in `drafted/general.yaml` as their own block in book order: after Chapter 7's block and before Chapter 10's first rule (`general.bc_1004_7_outdoor_areas`), so the Chapter 4 work (before 508), the Chapter 7 work and the Chapter 10 work touch other lines of the file. Pinned by `test_chapter_9_rules_sit_in_book_order_between_chapters_7_and_10` (packages/rule_format/tests/test_ny_building_chapter_9.py); Chapter 7's own order test now accepts Chapter 9's block after it. Labels and text verification entries go in book order too.
2. **A required system is a rule; how it is designed is a flag.** Where 903.2 requires a sprinkler system for the stated groups and the stated system is none, or one 903.3.1 doesn't permit there (a "lesser type": NFPA 13R outside Group R, NFPA 13D outside one- and two-family dwellings, Group R-3, Group R-4 Condition 1 and townhouses), the check fails. It passes only where the stated system is one the law permits for the stated groups. Where a fact it needs isn't stated (the groups, the system) or a permission's condition isn't in the model (903.3.1.2's heights above fire department vehicle access, Group R-4's or Group I-1's Condition, an I-4 day care's exterior doors), it needs review, never a pass. The system's design (Table 903.2.5.2's hazard classes, 13R's corridors and balconies, alternative systems) is a flag.
3. **The groups as facts.** A rule reads the building's one group (`occupancy_group`) and, for a building of two or more groups, whether any stated group is one 903.2 requires a system for whatever its size (new measures from the stated groups: `group_r_present`, `group_h_present`, `group_i1_i2_i3_present`), so a mixed building with a Group R, H or I-1 to I-3 occupancy and no sprinkler system fails. A building of two or more groups with an NFPA 13R or 13D system needs review: the one stated system can't say which portion has which.
4. **903.3.1.2's limits.** An NFPA 13R system is permitted "in Group R occupancies" four stories or fewer above grade plane, with the roof (Group R-2) or the highest floor (other Group R) within a height of the lowest level of fire department vehicle access, and the lowest floor within 30 ft below it. The stories are the architect's (`stories_above_grade_plane`, Revit's Gordium: Stories above grade plane, now also read by the Building Code's rules); the heights aren't in the model. So 13R in Group R needs review, and fails where the stated stories are more than four; 13R outside Group R fails where 903.2 requires a system (Group H, I-2, I-3), and needs review in Group I-1 (903.2.6 exception 1, Condition 1). 903.3.1.2 itself is a flag wherever a 13R system is stated or the system isn't: group 5's false pass (a Group B building with a 13R system given Chapter 10's sprinklered allowances) came from 13R outside Group R. Review round 1 found four Chapter 10 rules still counting it there (1007.1.1's story and space remoteness, 1007.1.2's, Group I-4's two exits, 1006.2.1): a Group B story's exits 0.4 of the diagonal apart passed at one-third. Each now counts a 13R system only in a building stated Group R and needs review between its limits otherwise, and a walk over every rule reading the 13R fact holds that no part passes with 13R outside Group R that doesn't pass with no system (`test_no_rule_passes_on_a_13r_system_outside_group_r`).
5. **New buildings.** 903.2 and 907.2 are for new buildings and structures; the check is of a new design (an existing building's alteration is the Existing Building Code's).

## Parts, in order

Each part: its pieces brought in, read a second time, labeled; its rules and flags; `packages/rule_format/tests/test_ny_building_chapter_9.py` (every line of each flagged piece quoted by its rule or flag, every section a piece names said in its flag's words, each rule walked over every group, sprinkler system and fact against an oracle written from the law: never a pass short of the strict reading, never a fail within the loosest); the four-unit apartment's check before and after.

1. **a: where a sprinkler system is required whatever the building's size, and which system: 903.1, 903.2 alone, 903.2.5 (Group H), 903.2.6 (Group I), 903.2.8 (Group R), 903.3, 903.3.1 and 903.3.1.1 to 903.3.1.3.** Status, drafting 2026-11-27.
2. **b (planned): the groups whose requirement turns on fire area, occupant load or story: 903.2.1 (A), 903.2.2 (B), 903.2.3 (E), 903.2.4 (F-1), 903.2.7 (M), 903.2.9 (S-1), 903.2.10 (S-2), 903.2.11 (every group but U), 903.2.12.** Fire areas aren't measured; a room lies wholly within one fire area, so a room whose own occupant load or floor area passes a limit settles it (a fail where none or a lesser system is stated), and a story's place settles "more than three stories above grade plane"; otherwise needs review. Status, drafted 2026-11-27: 903.2.1 to 903.2.4, 903.2.7, 903.2.9 and 903.2.10 with 15 definitions, 44 pieces read a second time (101 numbers, all agree; 31 comparisons the recorded words miss, mostly "exceeds", flagged). Eight per-room rules (A-1 to A-4, E, F-1, M, S-1), for a building stated as that one group: a room over the section's area or at its occupant load fails with less than NFPA 13 stated, NFPA 13 passes, any other room needs review (its fire area may be larger, on another floor, more than three stories above grade plane, or the group's fire areas together over 24,000 square feet: the stories condition is left to review, since "more than three stories above grade plane" may be read as from the fourth story or the fifth). 20 flags, each narrowed to its group, a building of two or more groups or not stated. The apartment: three more lines need review (an occupiable roof's assembly use, ambulatory care floors, bulk tires), nothing else changes. 903.2.11 and 903.2.12 move to part c with 907.

Two rules on the emergency voice/alarm system, a stated fact (`emergency_voice_alarm_system`), read on each story for a building stated as one group: Group A at an occupant load of 1,000 or more (907.2.1.1), Group E over 100 (907.2.3 exception 2); the occupancy's load is at least any one story's, so a story at the limit (both readings) with no system stated fails, a stated system passes, anything else needs review. 22 flags: no fire alarm fact is stated yet (the Revit-fields queue: `fire_alarm_system`, a manual fire alarm system per 907.2 and NFPA 72), so each group's manual fire alarm system and detection is a person's check. The apartment: 13 more lines need review (903.2.11's ten, 903.2.12, 907.1.3, 907.2, 907.2.2.1's ambulatory care while not ruled out), nothing else changes.
4. **d (planned): fire alarm and detection for Groups I, M, R and S, and smoke alarms (907.2.6 to 907.2.11), and the emergency voice/alarm communication system itself (907.5.2.2),** which 1005.3.1 and 1005.3.2's exception 1 already read as a stated fact (`emergency_voice_alarm_system`).
