**Purpose:** how the egress pieces that [2026-11-13_house_egress_rules.md](2026-11-13_house_egress_rules.md) left undrafted were made into rules, by the same method (its decisions 1 to 9), and what is still open.\

## Decisions

1. **Scope** (`book.SCOPE`, 22 more pieces, all labeled rule, all already brought in): R318.3, R318.3.1, R318.3.2 (landings at exterior doors and their floor drops); R318.7.3 (a flight's rise, 12 ft 7 in); R318.7.6 (stair landings); R318.7.11.1 (spiral stairs), R318.7.11.2 (bulkhead stairs); R318.7.12 to R318.7.12.2 (alternating tread devices) and R318.7.13 to R318.7.13.2 (ship's ladders); R318.8.1 to R318.8.3 (ramps); R319.4.2.1 and R319.4.2.2 (area well ladders and steps); R319.6 (additions); R320.3, R320.5, R320.6 (handrail projection, continuity, grip). R319.5, R319.5.1 and R319.7 are labeled flag (replacement windows and existing basements: judgments about existing work, as Appendix BO), so they stay flags in the labels and are not drafted.
2. **Measures first, by an engineer** (`parts.yaml`, one commit before the key): new part kinds `exterior_door`, `ramp`, `alternating_tread_device`, `ships_ladder`; new measures on stairs (rise, landings, spiral walkline, identical treads, bulkhead), handrails (projecting nosings, continuity, returns, grip profile; a handrail may now sit within a ramp), escape openings (ladder and steps sizes), sleeping rooms and basements (in an addition). A slope is rise over run (a count): "1 unit vertical in 12" is `ramp_slope * 12 <= 1`, the form the number check reads. The measuring code lists every new measure as not measured yet, so a rule reading one needs review, never pass, until it is measured.
3. **Answer key first.** The same kind of helper (no drafts, no records, no comparison seen) wrote 44 rules for the 22 pieces from the law, labels, second reading and parts list; committed before the drafting run (the commits show it).
4. **Drafting**: `python -m rule_drafting draft --state new_york/new_york_state --all --yes` (the 22 undrafted pieces), then `--only R318.7.3 R318.7.13.2` once more for the two refused. The drafter is now told which pieces are drafted, so an exception that sends a part to one of them (a spiral stairway to R318.7.11.1) leaves the part out rather than needing review, and how a slope is written (`book.BOOK_NOTE`). 53 recorded calls, **$2.11** (this book's drafting so far: 162 calls, $5.43; the shared ledger $15.70 of the $20 cap). The AI drafted 56 rules; code refused R318.7.3 and R318.7.13.2 three times each (only their examples' shape) and the means-of-egress rule of R318.7.12 and R318.7.13 (the number check doesn't read "200 gross square feet" as square feet); R318.7.11.2 it left without a rule (an exemption, nothing to check).
5. **The comparison** (`book_eval`): 92 new disagreements. Every one is resolved by the law in `resolutions.yaml`; every wrong draft is fixed, none left (`draft_fixes`, each change in the rule's `revised`, no AI spend). Of the 14 causes fixed, these could pass what the law fails: an alternating tread device or ship's ladder that is a space's only means of egress (nothing checked it: a rule added whole); a 13 ft flight and a ship's ladder's handrails (the refused answers, added by hand as the AI wrote them, examples corrected); a ramp at the egress door between 1 in 12 and 1 in 8 on the architect's word that 1 in 12 is infeasible (now needs review: a code official's judgment); and two found by reading the drafts, not by an example: a door onto a small balcony with a short landing inside (the depth given is the least of the two sides: needs review) and a sleeping loft's stair with a 12 in landing (the AI left loft stairs out: now fails under 24 in, needs review 24 to 36 in). The rest were stricter than the law or gave not applicable where R318.3 fails anyway (both fixed too). Fixes no worked example shows are each pinned by a test (`pinned_by`), which fails on the AI's own rules. After the fixes the disagreements left come from how examples are shaped or are drafts that only add needs review (a flag on every stair's riser slope; the floor-opening flag), each cause in `resolutions.yaml`.
6. **The spiral-stair placeholder is gone.** With R318.7.11.1's rules drafted and checked on both sides, the width, headroom, riser height and tread depth rules leave a spiral stair out (it is R318.7.11.1's), and the handrail and tread-variation rules hold it like any stair (R318.7.11.1 says nothing of them); the riser-variation rule needs review only above 3/8 in. Four key fixes and one draft fix, recorded. A spiral stair serving a sleeping loft that misses R318.7.11.1's numbers needs review (R315.5.2's loft limits may apply).

## Not yet

- **Format gaps** (the helper's and the drafter's): "200 gross square feet" isn't read as square feet by the number check, so the loft exception for alternating tread devices and ship's ladders needs review; "both sides" has no number, so their handrails are flags; a text measure can't be compared (`exterior_door_access_to_grade`), so R318.3.1's access to grade is a flag.
- **Bulkhead stairs** (R318.7.11.2): the new stair rules leave an exempt one out; the older ones (R318.7.1 to R318.7.8, R318.3's door rules) don't read the exemption yet, so an exempt bulkhead stair may fail wrongly (never pass wrongly).
- **Alternating tread device and ship's ladder handrails** aren't handrail parts (a handrail sits within a stair or a ramp), so R320.2's 30 to 34 in and R320.3 to R320.6 aren't checked on them: their handrail flags need review.
- **Measuring code**: none of the new measures or part kinds is measured yet; until the measuring code gives `exterior_door`, `ramp`, `alternating_tread_device` and `ships_ladder` parts (an empty list where the model has none), every rule for them needs review on a real model.
- Still undrafted (labeled rule): R318.1, R318.4, R318.6, R318.7.5.2.1 (winders), R318.7.5.3 (nosings), R318.7.7 (walking-surface slope), R319.1.1, R321.2.1, R321.2.2.
- Open for the architects (in the rules): R318.7.6, how far below a top door's threshold an interior stair's top landing may sit (only the garage case has a number); R320.5, whether a handrail end at a newel post or volute must also be returned; and the earlier ones (patio cover "unenclosed"; marked escape openings; may a loft stair mix R315.5.2's and R318.7's limits).
