# City of Ithaca, NY

The **City** of Ithaca, not the Town of Ithaca, which has its own zoning code.

- Zoning code: Chapter 325 of the City Code.
- Starting with residential districts: R-1a, R-1b, R-2a, R-2b, R-2c, R-3a, R-3aa, R-3b, RU.
- Note: the Southside ordinance (No. 2025-12, adopted Nov 2025) rezoned parts of some residential districts and is **not** reflected in the April 2026 zoning charts.

See `regulations/README.md` for what each numbered step means.

## Watch for changes

- The city is rewriting its zoning code ("Zone Ithaca", https://www.zoneithaca.com/). No adoption date announced as of 2026-10-19. When it is adopted, Chapter 325 and the charts change, and we re-run the steps.
- The city's zoning page (https://www.cityofithacany.gov/509/Zoning) is where new charts and maps appear. The charts and maps keep the same document number when updated, so re-check fingerprints occasionally.
